## For Seth's judgement

1. **gusen.org is the volunteer Gusen Memorial Committee** (citizen initiative; Martha
   Gammer, Rudolf Haunschmied), not the official memorial (gusen-memorial.org). It is
   long-standing and detailed, but its framing sometimes differs. If used, label it as a
   citizen initiative.
2. **The sources disagree on dates and numbers.** Gusen III opened on 16 or 26 Dec
   1944 (building from summer 1943). Prisoner numbers are given as about 260 (USHMM),
   274 (guides) or about 300 (Memorial). Brickworks forced labour started in 1940 or 1941. Any
   summary on the page shouldn't present one figure as settled.
3. **The ash's origin is "probably" / "with high likelihood"** the Gusen or Mauthausen
   crematoria. Keep that hedge.
4. **Sensitive content:** several of these pages describe human remains. Consider a short
   content note at the top of the page.
5. **Links likely to move:** the Bewusstseinsregion PDFs (under `wp-content/uploads/2024/12/`),
   the Mauthausen guides' bare `/node/305`, the "heimathaus" slug on Liberation Objects, and
   the subcamp map (no per-camp URL). Re-check these after any rebuild of those sites.
6. **Left out:** press items (tips.at, meinbezirk.at, OTS 2018), CBA radio, a
   temporary 2018 railway exhibition, the Katsdorf municipality site (nothing on its
   history), Austria-Forum (mirrors Wikipedia), Academia.edu, DÖW databases (nothing
   specific to Gusen III; optional). The publisher's own page for *BMÖ* 37 (oegm.or.at)
   couldn't be reached.
