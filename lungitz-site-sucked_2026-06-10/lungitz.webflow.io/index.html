Lungitz

Lungitz is a village in the community of Katsdorf, Perg district of Upper Austria. During WWII it was the site of slave labour and the “Gusen III” sub-camp of the Mauthausen-Gusen concentration camp complex.

The volume contains chronological handwritten entries, and historical photographs.

Das KZ -Lager Gusen III.

1997

/

Historical document

Leo Reichl / Heimatverein Katsdorf

No items found.

Leo Reichl’s text, Das KZ-Lager Gusen III, is a firsthand historical account detailing the establishment and operation of a Gusen subcamp in Lungitz, Upper Austria. Based on the author’s childhood observations and witness interviews, the text documents the construction of the camp in 1943, its industrial bakery designed to supply the Mauthausen complex, and the storage of aircraft components in a nearby brick factory. The text includes hand-drawn site plans and descriptions of the camp's physical layout. It also contains accounts of interactions between prisoner labor details and the local civilian population until the camp's dissolution in 1945. Although it is not a thorough, scientific account and was published only 50 years later, it is the most complete document of the camp's history to date and is widely cited in all scientific publications about Gusen 3.

„Zwangsarbeit der Insassen im KZ Gusen...“

2017

/

Scientific paper

Friedrich Ehn

No items found.

Focusing on the era of industrial mobilization and systematic exploitation, this thesis recounts the concentration camp's transition from early construction to high-capacity armament production. Key entries document the shift from SS-led stone quarrying for "annihilation through labor" to the large-scale underground relocation of the defense industry. It maintains a systematic record of the three-phase development and individual camp sectors alongside the final collapse and liberation of the Gusen complex.

Opfergrabstätte Lungitz

2021

/

Documentation

nextroom.at

No items found.

This document outlines the architectural design of a memorial site located next to the Gusen III concentration camp memorial stone. Following the discovery of ashes under the train tracks in 2018. Designed by Bernhard Denkinger, it combines archaeological preservation with a permanent structural marker. A Corten steel stele and a basalt-covered surface mark the burial ground, emphasizing the tension between historical burden and infrastructure.

Frühmittelalterliche Grabfunde aus Lungitz, Bezirk Perg, Oberösterreich

2021

/

Scientific paper

Felix Lettner, Michael Schwarz und Claudia Theune

No items found.

This scientific paper presents the archaeological findings of four early medieval burials discovered in 2018 during construction near the Lungitz train station in the Perg district of Upper Austria. These findings led to the discovery of a layer of ash that was identified as residue from the Mauthausen crematoriums.
